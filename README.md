# Comput

Generator for the brand's 0/1 graphics: circles are 0s, pills are 1s. Output is always SVG.

- **The Orb:** the sphere logomark, at any size, with centre diffusion (cross or hamburger).
- **Scene:** build illustrations from Comput's own 3D shapes (sphere, cube, cylinder, ring, capsule; each with its own width, height, depth and roundness, and any shape can cut into the layers above it; up to 24 layers). Layers can be shown or hidden (eye or H), renamed and reordered; hidden layers are left out of the preview, render and export with shapes that hide each other, cast shadows and an optional ground shadow. Drag shapes on the artboard, turn and tilt the camera, melt shapes together, start from ready-made scenes or save your own (saved in the browser).
- **Undo / redo:** buttons by Shuffle, or Cmd/Ctrl+Z and Shift+Cmd/Ctrl+Z (Ctrl+Y on Windows). Covers every setting and scene edit, up to 150 steps, for the current visit.
- **Tools under the canvas:** in Scene mode the dock under the artboard holds Move, Rotate and Scale, the shapes (sphere, cube, cylinder, ring, capsule), the Pen, and **Clear**, which removes every layer. In Picture mode it holds Choose image, Use sample and **Clear**, which removes the picture. Undo brings back anything cleared, and a cleared picture stays cleared after a reload.
- **Pen (draw your own shape):** in Scene mode, pick the **Pen** in the dock. Click for a corner point; click and drag to pull out handles for a curve (hold Option/Alt while dragging to move only the outgoing handle). Click the first point or press Enter to close; Backspace removes the last point, Esc cancels. The new layer has Depth and **Inflate** (0 = flat cut-out, higher = puffed up like a balloon) and works with every tool.
- **Edit points:** right after closing a shape, or by double-clicking a drawn layer (or **Edit points** in the Scene panel): drag points and handles, click the outline to add a point (the curve keeps its shape), double-click or Option/Alt-click a point to switch it between corner and curve, Option/Alt-drag a point to pull out new handles, Option/Alt-drag a handle to break the pair, Delete removes the selected point, arrows nudge it. Enter, Esc or **Done** finishes. Works from any camera angle. Older drawings convert to curves with exactly the same shape.
- **Wire / Rendered views (W):** Wire shows outlines, creases and a floor grid only, instant for moving around and drawing; Rendered shows the 0s and 1s. Exports always use the full render.
- **Scene controls:** drag a shape to edit it with the current tool (Move, Rotate, Scale; Free or X, Y, Z). Click empty space to deselect; drag empty space to pan. Right-drag or Option/Alt-drag to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Keys: G move, R rotate, S scale, X/Y/Z axis, Esc free, F reset view, Delete remove, Shift+D duplicate, arrows nudge.
- **Speed:** the engine runs in a background worker, so the page never waits on it. In Scene mode a GPU preview (`src/gpu.js`) draws the same lighting and 0/1 marks live at full frame rate while you work; the exact render replaces it a moment after you stop.
- **Logo tab (the logo generator):** Comput always opens here. The company's logo, locked to the designer's original settings. Changes made in The Orb never reach it. Pick a size from 64 px to 10K (8K and 10K for print and posters) and a colourway (Black, White, Black on white, White on black, or any colours in the Colour panel), then export: SVG (For web is about 8 MB at 10K; For Figma is much heavier above 4K) or PNG at the full size (drawn in strips and streamed into the file, so 10K works in every browser; about 21 MB). The tab is standardised: no Shuffle, no Responsive or Motion, and the arrangement (seed 3) is fixed. The preview always fits the screen; the size sets the exported file and the grain. Bigger sizes get more, finer marks, so it always reads as particles. A 10K logo is about 437,000 marks and takes around 10 seconds.
- **PNG export** in every tab, at the full size.
- **The original logo, as a particle texture:** The Orb uses every setting from the designer's `sphere_logo_dense.py` (4,200 marks, each 1/80 of the ball wide, the same light, pills and seed 3, pure black on transparent). At 96 px, the size it was shared at, it is the shared logo exactly. Bigger sizes add marks and keep each one fine, so it always reads as a particle texture, never as obvious pills and circles. The **Original** look puts every one of those settings back.
- **Looks:** built-in looks (Original, Ink, Paper, Night, Fine, Bold, Photo, Metal) and your own. Export saved looks and scenes as one `comput-library.json` for the team, and import it on another computer.
- **Motion:** Build up, Light orbit, Diffusion (logo) and Turn (scenes). Frames start from the previous frame's marks so they drift smoothly. Export as video (MP4 or WebM, depending on the browser), a ZIP of SVG frames, or for Build up one animated SVG.
- **Describe a scene:** only in the Claude artifact, where it runs on the viewer's own Claude plan (no API credit). It is hidden on brangic.studio. Prompts live in `src/prompts.js`. Claude's most capable model builds the object from the shape library in real proportions; a fast, inexpensive model checks the render, and the capable model runs again only to fix what the check found. The public site has no AI connection.
- **Picture:** any picture or 3D render turned into 0s and 1s. Dark areas pack tight, light areas stay sparse, outlines get extra packing, and pills follow the shapes. Cut-out PNGs with transparent backgrounds work best.
  - **3D form** (optional): *Inflate* rounds out any flat cut-out shape, no 3D software needed; or load a *normal map* exported from 3D software. Pills then follow the surface (Burst, Follow, Rings, like the logo), shorten towards the edges, and Comput can light the object with The Orb's own light, shadow and rim model. Illustrations keep their own light, separate from the logo's.

- Live: https://brangic.studio/comput
- `src/engine.js` is the generator, ported from `sphere_logo_dense.py`, with a size-aware scaling law and centre diffusion, plus the image path (tone mapping, outline packing, a flow field for pill direction).
- `src/app.html` is the interface, `src/worker.js` runs the engine off the page, `src/gpu.js` is the live scene preview.
- `src/build.sh` puts the engine into the page and writes `docs/comput/index.html` (served by GitHub Pages) and `comput.html` (the Claude artifact version).

After editing anything in `src/`, run `src/build.sh`, then commit and push. Pages republishes on its own.
